Platform reliability

Risk-based validation for Databricks upgrades

My role: Designed and led; adopted across engineering teams

Read the article ↗ Read the post ↗

The Challenge

Testing enterprise SQL warehouse upgrades by re-running ~1,800 UAT DAGs was ad hoc, slow, and expensive.

The Solution

Built a 3-tier validation framework (~150 EXPLAIN probes + GitHub Actions) that scans repos and tests syntax and functions against new runtimes without running production jobs.

The Impact

  • Time & Cost: Saved ~2 weeks of testing time and ~$10K in compute per upgrade run, delivering full-repo compile coverage in 5 minutes.
  • Community: Published publicly so any Databricks team can validate SQL syntax before runtime upgrades.

Modernization · Cost & speed

Survey platform migration to Databricks

My role: Technical lead for the migration

Read the post ↗

The Challenge

A legacy survey pipeline fragmented across Presto, Hive, Postgres, and SFTP caused 4+ week change cycles and required manual holiday ops freezes.

The Solution

Consolidated workflows into one modular PySpark notebook on Databricks with automated exclusion rules, code-driven holiday pauses, and direct Ops alerting.

The Impact

  • Speed & Savings: Cut change request turnaround from 4+ weeks to under 1 week and decommissioned legacy Postgres to save $3K+/year.
  • Business Value: Extended survey coverage to unhandled calls, unlocking interaction reports for targeted manager coaching.
